Pick the next game together.

One shared list for your group. Add games, vote them up or down, mark what you already own, and see what it would cost — for you, and for everyone.

Sign in through Steam
  • A single list everybody can see and argue about
  • Prices, Metascores and Steam review summaries in one row
  • Three totals: the whole list, your share, and the group's bill
  • Invite links with roles — permission lives on the server, not in the browser

Add

Search the Steam store and drop a game on the list. Prices follow the crew's region.

Vote

“yes please” or “hard no”. One hard no is enough to take a game out, and the list sorts itself so the group's favourite floats up.

Settle up

Mark what you own. playpile works out who still has to buy what, and what that costs.
